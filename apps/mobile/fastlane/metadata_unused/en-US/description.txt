SwimHub is the all-in-one app for swimmers and coaches to track training, races, and team activity in one place.

Whether you swim for a club, a school team, or on your own, SwimHub turns every practice and meet into clear, comparable data so you can see your progress at a glance.

PRACTICE & RACE LOG
• Record every practice session with sets, distances, and notes
• Log race results and split times for each stroke and distance
• Keep your best times up to date automatically

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• A calendar dashboard shows practices and competitions at a glance
• Track how your times improve over the season
• Review detailed splits for any race

BUILT FOR TEAMS
• Manage rosters and team membership
• Track attendance and aggregate entries for competitions
• Share schedules so everyone stays in sync

MADE FOR SWIMMERS
• Times shown in clean minutes:seconds.hundredths format
• Standard event names and distances
• Works offline so you can log poolside, anywhere
