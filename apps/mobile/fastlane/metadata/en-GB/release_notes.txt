We've reorganised the app so the features you use most are quicker to reach.

HOME
- "Action needed" and team announcements are now combined into a single "Updates" card. Each team's to-dos, such as attendance you haven't answered yet or meets open for entries, are visible at a glance
- Announcements are listed by title. Tap one to read it in a pop-up
- The "Next meet" card is easier to read. Tap a target time to open the goal's details
- Bulk best time entry has moved into the Best Times table on Home. Enter times right in the table, with the input field sitting just above the keyboard

TEAMS
- Team tabs are now organised into four: Members, Schedule, Attendance and More. The number and order of tabs stay the same in admin view
- Practices and meets are combined in the Schedule tab, with filters for type and past events
- Open announcements and team settings from the icons in the header
- The Attendance tab shows all upcoming practices and meets in one list. Tap Present, Absent or Other and your response is saved straight away
- Admins can switch events between Open and Closed in one go with "Change multiple", and can correct members' responses directly

ACCOUNT
- My Page and Settings are combined into a new Account tab. Plan, Calendar, Practice log templates and Sign-in & security are all reachable from here

FIXES & IMPROVEMENTS
- Fixed an issue where turning off auto-renewal for a subscription removed Premium access even though the paid period hadn't ended
- "No response" is now counted the same way on Home and in Teams
- Loading placeholders now match the layout of each screen
